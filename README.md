# Moondrop Mountain

A tiny farm game for Orion. You look down at a spring farm on Moondrop Mountain: walk the fields, plant a seed, talk to Junie, watch a chicken wander, step inside the farmhouse, follow the dirt path north to a mine, or walk east into a small town.

Art is **original 16-bit 3/4** (2.5D): you see the top of the ground and the front of walls, roofs, crates, and people. It is meant to feel like the same *family* as Stardew Valley. It does **not** copy or trace Stardew sprites, the farmhouse, UI, or characters.

## Open it

Public URL (GitHub Pages):

https://yamizzle.github.io/orion-farm/

On a Chromebook, iPad, or iPhone: open that link in the browser (Safari or Chrome). Landscape on iPad is the intended view.

On iPhone, tap the tiny wood **FS** plaque (top-right) so the farm fills the Safari page. Tap **X**, or swipe down from the top, to go back. Add to Home Screen for a chrome-free farm.

- Tap the grass or path to walk there. On a keyboard, hold W A S D or the arrow keys to walk. The camera follows so you stay in the middle until you reach the edge of the mountain.
- Tap the farmhouse door or the stoop in front of it to go inside. Walk onto the doormat (or tap the inside door) to come back out, just south of the house.
- The mailbox by the house raises a teal flag and a letter when mail is waiting. Tap it (walk up if you need to) to read a wood-and-parchment letter. After you read it the flag goes down; tap again to re-read. A second letter arrives after the first mine visit. A third letter arrives after you leave wood at Nim's note.
- The first hotbar slot is a seed packet. Tap an empty brown plot or a hole you dug to plant a sprout. It grows over a day: sprout, then a young plant, then a ready carrot next morning. Tap a ready crop to pick it. Tap a planted flower to pick it back up.
- Tall grass stands on the wild fields and the town meadows. Tap a clump to mow it, or swing the cave sword (Space / J / the sword button) to cut a small arc. Sometimes a flower or a coin pops out. Wild tall grass grows back after about a day; meadow grass stays short once cut.
- Craft a **shovel** at the house workbench (4 wood, 2 stone). Select it on the hotbar, then tap grass to dig a hole. Holes stay; plant in them with the seed packet. Digging costs a little energy.
- Tap a tree or a rock from the next tile. **Each tap is one hit.** Hands take five; select the **axe** or **pick** for two (mine rocks are faster with a pick). Chips stay if you walk away; the trunk or stone looks more broken as it wears down. The right tool shows in Orion's hand. Wood and stone **drop on that tile** — walk onto the pile to pick it up. A stump or smashed rock comes back after about a day unless you built or dug there.
- Wood is the second hotbar slot (2 wood = one fence). Stone is the third (1 stone = one path). Tap the slot, then tap empty grass or dirt to build. Fences block walking; paths do not.
- Tap Junie in the straw hat. She has a small job for you. If you have a carrot, she buys it for 10G and says thanks. Gold shows on the clock plaque. Tap again to close the box.
- A chicken wanders the farm grass. Tap it and it hops.
- Follow the dirt path north to the timbered cave in the rocky hill. Walk onto the mouth to go inside; tap the south ladder to come back out.
- Inside, Nim left a wall note and three treasures in different rooms: a copper nugget, a cave mushroom, and a moon shard. Walk onto them to pick them up.
- A wood-framed hole with a down arrow sits at the north end of floor 1. Walk onto it (or tap it) the same way as the cave mouth to drop to floor 2, then again to floor 3. Each deeper floor has a ladder back up. The HUD clock says MINE 1, MINE 2, or MINE 3.
- Floor 2 is a darker winding cave: six foes (one slightly tougher slime), rocks to smash, and a teal gem that gives a copper nugget and a little gold. Floor 3 is a small deep chamber with more bats and grubs, a bigger five-hit slime, and Nim's moon crystal in a chest.
- A wood star on the HUD opens your stars. The first time you earn one, a big star card pops in the middle of the farm. Tap OK to keep going.
- A wood book next to the star is your job list. Tap it for one line, like BRING A MUSHROOM TO JUNIE. The book also has **MUTE** / **SOUND** for the chip audio (on after the first tap).
- Jobs: bring Junie a cave mushroom, leave wood at Nim's note after you read the mine letter, then take a moon shard inside the house. After it comes home the shard sits on the table as the Home Light and the lamp feels warmer. The job book then says to plant flowers with Lila. After three flowers on the town green and her thanks, it says to bring copper to Pip. After you hand Pip one copper, a path lantern sits by the shop door and the book says to help Reed's car. Talk to Reed, tap the red car (it beeps), then talk to Reed again. Two tiny amber lamps sit on the car at night after that, and the book says the town statue is still dark. After the peak light, Nim (a shy moon-kid, not a ghost) waits by the statue at night. Talk: thanks, the mountain can shine. That unlocks **Moondrop Night** — extra town lantern glow, sparkles, a star, and a brighter night jig. The book then says MOONDROP NIGHT SHINES. Start over clears it.
- The wood clock on the right counts real days. Morning turns to afternoon, evening (warm dusk), then night (a deep blue-purple grade over the farm, with path lanterns and house windows lighting the road; the house stays lamplit). Lanterns stay dark through evening dusk and only come on when night starts. A full day is about two and a half minutes. Time pauses while you read a letter, open stars, watch a new star unlock, sleep, faint, and when the tab is hidden.
- Inside the house, tap the bed to sleep. The room fades, then you wake next to the bed on the next morning with full hearts and energy. Crops and chopped trees keep growing while you rest. The farm remembers your day, bag, gold, plants, and where you stood if you close the page.
- A chest sits by the workbench. Tap it to store wood, stone, carrots, flowers, buns, and treasures. The bag holds 99 of each; extras you already had move into the chest. Tap the bag side of a row to put one in, the chest side to take one out, or PUT ALL / TAKE ALL. When the bag is full, pickups say BAG FULL. USE THE CHEST.
- The job book has a small **Start over** button. It asks "Really start over?" Tap Yes to wipe the save and reload a fresh farm; tap No to keep playing. A **MUTE** button sits under it.
- Follow the dirt path east from the farm (south of the house) to a small town: cobble streets, a green with a fountain and moon-kid statue, three houses, and Pip's shop. Town houses answer a knock; the shop door opens an interior you can buy from.
- Town folk: **Pip** keeps the shop, **Lila** waters the green, **Reed** watches the red car. Tap them to talk. Lila wants three flowers on the town green (the meadow around the fountain and statue). Pip wants one copper for a lantern by the shop door — tap Pip in town or at the shop counter; if you have copper it is handed over. Reed wants you to say hi to the car so it can have night lamps. The car loops the cobble and waits if you stand in the road; tap it and it beeps.
- In the shop, spend gold: seed tin 5G (extra garden plots), flower 8G, honey bun 6G, straw hat 25G, fence kit 12G, path kit 8G. Sell a carrot for 10G. Flowers plant on grass or meadow (select the flower, then tap a walkable grass or meadow tile). Three flowers on the town green count for Lila; farm plots do not. Tap a honey bun to eat it (heals two hearts / 20 HP and 25 energy); tap a carrot in the hotbar to eat it (one heart / 10 HP and 9 energy, "YUM! A CARROT."). Quest mushrooms and moon shards cannot be eaten. Tap the hat to put it on or take it off.
- Five red hearts sit on the HUD (50 HP). The green E bar is real energy (100). Chopping, rock-smashing, and sword swings each cost a little; a long walk costs a tiny bit. Old saves start full. Sleeping refills both.
- Just inside the mine, a stone pedestal holds a cave sword. Walk up and tap it. A round wood-and-gold sword button appears at the lower-right; tap it (or press Space / J) to swing an arc in front of Orion. Slimes take two hits; bats and rock-grubs take three. Touch-first; WASD still only walks. Tapping a foe still works as a bonus.
- Cute slimes, bats, and rock-grubs wander the mine rooms only. They bump Orion for one heart, then he flashes for a moment. They do not chase across the whole cave, and they stay off the ladder and room doors. Defeat one for a little gold (sometimes a stone). First foe unlocks the CAVE FOE star.
- If hearts or energy hit zero, a short 16-bit "YOU DIED..." card plays (letterbox, stars, wood plaque). Then Orion wakes in his house bed on the next morning with full hearts and energy. The file keeps going — bag, sword, gold, quests, and mine progress stay. The mountain takes a 10% gold toll (rounded down, minimum 0) and says THE MOUNTAIN SENT YOU HOME. A refresh after that wake does not put him back in the mine at 0 HP.
- Sound is original Web Audio chip-tune (no MP3/OGG): pulse leads, triangle bass, noise hats. The first pointer, touch, or key calls `audioCtx.resume()` so iPhone home-screen apps can play. After that, quiet loops follow the scene (farm day, farm night, house, mine, festival) and tiny SFX play for chop, smash, swing, hit, pickup, talk, sleep, craft, UI, faint, eat, and star toasts. Same loop is not restarted every frame. Mute from the job book; default is on after the first tap.


## Where the art lives

```
assets/
  ART.md            art contract for both harnesses (read this first)
  art.json          specials only (families, flips). Sizes live in the manifest
  refs/             frozen style sheet + Orion turnaround
  inbox/            proposed Imagine PNGs (not loaded by the game)
  generated-src/    historical Imagine dumps (do not add new work here)
  palette.json      named hex colors (grassMid, woodMid, …)
  manifest.json     every sprite: id, file, size, frames, anchor, atlas rect
  atlas.png         packed sheet the game loads
  atlas.json        same frame rects (handy for tools)
  tiles/            shipped 16×16 ground
  props/            shipped house, tree, well, cave, furniture, town, house chest, …
  actors/           shipped Orion, Junie, Nim, chicken, town folk, car, foes
  ui/               shipped items, hearts, stars, slash
tools/
  art.py                one-sprite preview / promote / restore (use this)
  process-generated.py  old batch pass; requires --all
  export-assets.py      pack shipped PNGs into atlas.png (do not use --regen)
  town_sprites.py       paint town houses, folk, car, shop goods
  combat_sprites.py     paint hearts, sword, pedestal, gold coin, mine foes, faint stars, slash sparks
  mine_sprites.py       paint the down-hole, gem, and chest
  hero_sprites.py       paint Orion, Junie, Nim, moon-kid statue, moon shard, mountain heart, faint pose
```

The game loads `assets/manifest.json` + `assets/atlas.png`, then draws with `Assets.draw(ctx, id, x, y, frame)`. It does not paint world pixels in the game loop.

## How to change a graphic

Read `assets/ART.md`. Short version:

1. Generate (edit-chain from `assets/refs/`) and drop `assets/inbox/<id>.png`.
2. `python3 tools/art.py preview <id>` — compare strip in `assets/preview/`, farm unchanged.
3. When it looks right: `python3 tools/art.py promote <id>` — writes that sprite (or its family) and packs the atlas.
4. If it does not: `python3 tools/art.py restore <id>`.

Do not run `process-generated.py` without `--all`. Do not run `export-assets.py --regen`.

To add a **new** id, first put it in `assets/manifest.json`:

```json
{ "id": "tomato", "file": "ui/tomato.png", "w": 16, "h": 16, "frames": 1, "anchor": [0, 0] }
```

Then inbox → preview → promote as above, and draw it with `Assets.draw(ctx, "tomato", x, y)`.

## How to update later

Edit files in this repo and push to `main`. GitHub Pages usually finishes deploy in under a minute. Then tap the wood **RL** plaque (top-right) or **RELOAD** in the job book — that reloads with a fresh `?v=` so Safari and Home Screen apps drop the old file. A hard browser refresh (Cmd-Shift-R) works too, but only after Pages has actually published.
